About

Two sisters, one big heart

Hi, we're Riverside Rebels! We're two sisters from Columbia, Tennessee, and we write songs about the stuff life is made of: love, laughter, heartbreak, fresh starts and the ones who stand by us.

We hope our songs feel like a front-porch talk with an old friend. Thanks for being here. It means the world to us.
